# Judge without letting hype cancel a veto
**Input:** Confirmed mint, [rules.json](../../rules.json), as-of times, public risk evidence and selected profile. All thresholds are starter assumptions to test, **not probabilities**. Choose the profile before looking at results.

1. Hard policy failures (`FAIL`): mismatched program, live mint/freeze authority, recognized restrictive transfer control, concentration beyond the caps or documented false project identity. **REJECT** under this policy, not an allegation of fraud. Missing data, unsupported modes/extensions, a severe vendor warning not adjudicated, low real depth or a missing sell route → **WATCH**, not “safe”.
2. Chain gates: finalized matching create no older than 30 min; supported conventional active native-SOL curve and at least 2 real SOL; full owner reconciliation; largest ≤15%, top ten ≤60%, recorded creator + create-user ≤10% of non-curve float. Require a usable RugCheck report; keep named warnings. These strict caps can exclude nearly every coin in the first few slots.
3. Activity gates: at least five observed non-role buy wallets, two sell wallets, three distinct slots across both sides (up to the last 16 curve signatures within 180 s). Wallets are not people. Bounded pre-launch role-wallet history must be available; direct role funding of a sampled buyer or repeated recent launches → review. A fresh, supplied review with a documented conflict and two distinct references can block **any** profile; the scanner does not search for conflicts on its own. A missing history sample at this checkpoint cannot pass. A rate limit outside this checkpoint can stop collection or prevent a fill instead.
4. Strategy profiles: `flow` uses no GitHub or social bonus. `builder` ALSO requires independent mint binding, verified release and working demo. `social` ALSO requires mint binding, two dated follower snapshots/growth, independent sources and paid/organic separation. Follow the [profile-review handoff](../../notes/profile-review.md). `--reviews DIR` selects a **directory**, not the file; save a copy of the [review template](../../templates/review.json) named for the exact mint. Supply distinct references and a fresh independent review. The DB does not remember the profile; specify it on both `scan` and `recheck`. `holder` is a later research track, not a minute-zero entry switch: review at 1d/7d, check returning users, shipped work, concentration, unlock schedule and post-migration exit liquidity. Keep its ledger separate; no AMM entry adapter ships here.
5. Check cash, one-position/one-entry limits, fresh full-size two-way route and modeled round-trip cost ≤5%. At least one leg must include the verified curve on entry and on the signal-time reverse probe; the code does not require 100% routing through that curve. The delayed buy is freshly quoted, not followed by a new reverse probe at the delayed quantity. See [route and size evidence](../../notes/technical-audit.md#3-check-the-route-and-the-amount-being-reversed). Quote limit is 1%; extra execution haircut is 0.30% per side. Missing info never earns points. The decision expires; [Paper trade](../paper-trade/SKILL.md) re-quotes after a delay.

**Output:** `DECISION` records the assessed gates, reasons and policy hash. If creation is outside the age window it may list **only creation**. Otherwise later flow/history/quote gates skipped by an earlier check are marked `NOT_RUN`. An absent or skipped gate is not a pass. Any `FAIL` → `REJECT`; otherwise any non-pass → `WATCH`; all pass → `PAPER_ELIGIBLE`. Never turn a project score into a buy recommendation.

**Kit location:** Run commands from the kit root, not this skill folder. See the [operator setup](../../README.md) and [agent routing map](../../AGENTS.md). `SOLANA_LAB_ROOT` is an optional agent convention; the Python tools do not read it.
