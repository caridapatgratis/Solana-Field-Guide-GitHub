# Design the project before the coin
**Input:** Who the project serves, the meme or IP you own, budget, skills, time, intended venue, country of promotion and conflicts. Ask about unknown essentials; do not fill them with invented claims.

1. Write one sentence a community member could repeat without a price prediction. Explain what is here today and what is only planned. Check rights to the name, art and upstream code. A fan project is not an official token.
2. Choose a primary audience and a success measure. **Fast traders:** ability to discover the right mint and get a real-size two-way quote. **Builder buyers:** a usable release and a verified link to the mint. **Social/narrative traders:** original, time-stamped posts, with paid posts labeled. **Holders:** returning users, next release, treasury reports and unlocks. One audience is enough to start. Do not use an opening price spike as the shared goal.
3. Choose a minimum product that works without a purchase. Example: a browser meme remix tool with an asset license, a public example and an export button. A pure community meme can skip code: say so. Explain whether the token actually has a role. If none, do not imply revenue rights or necessary utility.
4. Run a small pre-token test. Let several outsiders try the demo unaided. Count completed tasks and people who return a week later. State in advance what would make you keep building without a coin. Budget separately for development, moderation, monitoring, any liquidity capital and legal review.
5. List the strongest counterexample: would the audience still care if there were no ticker or chart? If the only answer is “bots will buy”, return **NOT READY**. Do not buy stars, trade against yourself or pay for undisclosed calls.

**Output:** a working brief copied from [templates/launch-brief.md](../../templates/launch-brief.md); include chosen audience, real use, evidence today, honest token role, 7/30-day measures, cost cap, rights and go/no-go owner. Hand off to [Launch rehearsal](../launch-rehearsal/SKILL.md). No market actions.
