# Research notes — 18 November 2026
The sources answer different questions. Research gives a base rate or a warning; API documentation says what can actually be observed; neither provides a guaranteed trading rule. Thresholds in [rules.json](../rules.json) are our starting hypotheses, not statistics from these papers.

## What the studies actually measured
| Evidence | Finding and limit | Design decision |
|---|---|---|
| CoinGecko, Pump.fun lifespan [1](https://www.coingecko.com/research/publications/average-lifespan-of-pumpfun-tokens) | Of 18.67m traded Pump.fun launches from Jan 14 2024 through Jun 18 2026, 68.67% had their last **recorded curve trade** on launch day. It excluded zero-trade mints and did not track activity after migration. This is not a death rate for all coins. | Start the sample at creation, keep quiet launches and follow migrations. |
| Szwajcok et al., *Meme Coin Factories*, CCS 2026 [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10246) | Studied the population of 15m Pump.fun creations and transaction samples; found wash trading, obscured creator addresses, coordinated sells, copies and social manipulation. This does not mean every concurrent buy is linked. | Separate signer, payer and creator; label funding links as evidence, not human identity. |
| Mongardini & Mei, *A Midsummer Meme's Dream*, v2 [3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01963) | Cross-chain data for 34,988 memecoins. Among their high-return (>100%) tokens, 82.8% showed signs of artificial growth. A selected subset across four chains, **not** 82.8% of Solana mints. | A “mooners” list is a poor sampling frame. Record tradeable size and exits. |
| He et al., ICSE 2026, suspected fake GitHub stars [1](https://cmustrudel.github.io/papers/icse2026fakestars.pdf) | The detector flagged about six million suspected fake stars. It studies GitHub generally, not memecoin buyers. | Ignore raw stars as an entry condition; inspect provenance, original changes, release and actual use. |
| Lucchini et al., *Science Advances*, 2020 [1](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.abd2204); [open-access text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7744071/) | Studied common developers and later correlations between cryptocurrencies' returns. This does not estimate repo-link conversion for new Solana mints. | Separate association and acquisition. Do not extrapolate a buyer percentage. |
| MELT, Solana high-risk launch dataset [1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13480v2) | Examines behavioral features including early holding concentration during its sample window. Not evidence that our thresholds are profitable. | Include concentration and actual trading as distinct checkpoints; test out of time. |

**Conclusion about GitHub:** In the sources reviewed I did not find a defensible universal share of early Solana buyers motivated by a GitHub repo, or a reliable ranking of repo genres by buyer conversion. A wallet swap does not carry a “why I bought” field. Check the specific project and run a prospective test. See the [GitHub study protocol](study.md).

## Primary technical sources and how to use them
- **Mint and permissions.** Solana's token quickstart, authority roles, and permanent delegate docs distinguish normal supply controls from powers to move/burn a holder's tokens. Check extensions as well as mint/freeze keys. [1](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/quickstart) [1](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/basics/set-authority) [1](https://solana.com/docs/tokens/extensions/permanent-delegate) [2](https://solana.com/news/token-extensions-developer-guide)
- **Creation and market stage.** Pump’s official public docs define creation, quote choices, modes and migration; use the official program, not a token suffix. Raydium documents its curve-to-pool launch flow and the difference between locked liquidity and the right to collect fees. [1](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs) [4](https://docs.raydium.io/user-flows/launchlab-overview) [1](https://docs.raydium.io/user-flows/burn-and-earn). Implementation pin: [pinned Pump interface definition](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/639b87fb45c3adeffa6f7c4a1bf0cb85e392bf22/idl/pump.json).
- **RPC scope.** A `logsSubscribe` mentions filter takes one address; a transaction may be absent at the requested commitment. `getTokenLargestAccounts` returns up to 20 **accounts**, not all beneficial owners. Keep gaps, dedupe and reconcile. [4](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/websocket/logssubscribe) [3](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/gettransaction) [1](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http/gettokenlargestaccounts) [2](https://solana.com/docs/rpc/http)
- **Rug report and listings.** RugCheck's [official API schema](https://api.rugcheck.xyz/swagger/doc.json) exposes reports and insider graphs; the score is a vendor opinion, not a guarantee. DEX Screener exposes per-mint pools, volumes, boosts and paid-order endpoints. Paid boosts can increase its Trending Score; they do not establish organic demand. [4](https://docs.dexscreener.com/api/reference) [1](https://docs.dexscreener.com/boosting)
- **Quotes and fees.** A Jupiter V2 order without `taker` is a quote, not an order that has landed. It exposes input/output amounts, minimums, route and impact. Routed fees are reflected in the quote; separate modeled network/priority costs from those fees. Solana charges fees for landed failed transactions too. [1](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/swap) [2](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/api-reference/swap/order) [1](https://solana.com/docs/core/fees/fee-structure)
- **Repo evidence.** GitHub supplies fork lineage and repo metadata; release `published_at` is distinct from the underlying commit date. Build attestations establish origin, not quality. Repository traffic with appropriate access is not a buyer list. [1](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/repos/repos) [3](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/releases/releases) [3](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/concepts/supply-chain-security/supply-chain-security) [2](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/viewing-activity-and-data-for-your-repository)
- **Public attention.** X's user lookup exposes account birthday and current follower metrics; use dated snapshots for growth. Recent search is time-limited. The trends endpoint is location-specific. Telegram bots get updates from channels they belong to; standard incoming updates are retained for at most 24 hours, and edits are distinct. These sources are not permission to scrape private data. [1](https://docs.x.com/x-api/users/lookup/integrate) [4](https://docs.x.com/make-your-first-request) [1](https://docs.x.com/x-api/trends/trends-by-woeid/introduction) [1](https://core.telegram.org/bots/faq) [3](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api)
- **Local promotion.** If raising money or marketing to people in Indonesia, obtain current local legal advice first. OJK is the digital-financial-asset supervisor; its guidance for licensed traders emphasizes risk warnings, no misleading material and no guaranteed returns. This does **not** mean a private issuer is automatically licensed or subject to exactly the same rules. Technical trading rules were updated in 2026. [2](https://ojk.go.id/en/berita-dan-kegiatan/siaran-pers/Pages/Bappebti-Transfers-Regulation-and-Supervision-Duties-on-Digital-Financial-Assets-Crypto-Assets-and-Derivatives-to-OJK-BI.aspx) [4](https://www.ojk.go.id/en/berita-dan-kegiatan/pengumuman/Documents/PENG-7%20PT%20Utama%20Aset%20Digital%20Indonesia%20Business%20License.pdf) [1](https://ojk.go.id/id/regulasi/Pages/PADK-Nomor-3-Tahun-2026-Penyelenggaraan-Perdagangan-Aset-Keuangan-Digital-termasuk-Aset-Kripto.aspx)

The two live examples bundled here are our own dated observations, not findings from the studies. No authenticated X or Telegram data, hidden identities or simulated 'historical' social signals were used.

For the difference between a historical market study and this kit’s two small demonstrations, see the [recorded runs and receipts](../examples/README.md). Source sites require internet and may rate-limit automated requests; the local material does not.
